- **`ADR-0131` is NOT reverted and `echo`/`hcm` are NOT touched.** The first-byte rule is real; it governs the RBAC **verdict**, not the chain's hand-off to the terminal filter. That distinction is the whole of C-1.

The behavioral half was sound the whole time, which is exactly why nobody looked.

That is **M-2's failure mode, encountered one task before M-2 was fixed.** Every read in the backstop file, and the config-validation helper itself, are now bounded.
- **A nondeterministic panic passes on the first run.** Task 18's `PendingTasks` rewiring left the in-crate `mk_multi_socket_listener` helper with ONE slot and N sockets; `reuseport_fanout_serves_and_drains` panicked `index out of bounds` on a `tokio-rt-worker` **only when the kernel steered the connection to socket #2**.
