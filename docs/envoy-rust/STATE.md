> Authored at the phase-28 state-1 brainstorm (`superpowers:brainstorming`). Records WHY phase 28 is the `RING_HASH` LB opener + the alternatives weighed; the full reasoning is **ADR-0069**. (Relocates to `STATE_HISTORY.md` per ADR-0035 / §4.1 inv. 9 at the phase-28 state-6 close-out, when row `28` flips `done`.)

- **The pick OPENS a new family (Load balancing), departing the six-phase xDS arc.** The project shipped only `ROUND_ROBIN` (phase 02); the LB family (`§9`: least_request/random/ring_hash/maglev/subset/locality-weighted/priority) is foundational — subset/locality/priority LB all layer onto a real policy-dispatch + a request-hash plumbing this phase builds.
