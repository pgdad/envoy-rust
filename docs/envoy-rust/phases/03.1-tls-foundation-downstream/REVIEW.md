The catch-all is a nice forward-compatibility touch — `pki_types::ServerName` is `#[non_exhaustive]` in 1.x; future variants would otherwise compile-fail this match, but the explicit catch-all means the enum extends gracefully (rejection is the safe default for an upstream-side accept-list).
